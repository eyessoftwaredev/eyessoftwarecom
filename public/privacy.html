Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This document is available in English only.

1. Who we are

Eyes Software FZCO (“Eyes Software”, “we”, “us”) operates eyessoftware.com and is responsible for the personal data described in this policy.

2. Data we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address and the message you send us.
  • Projects and invoices: your name, company name (if any), billing address, email address, phone number, tax registration number where applicable, and the details of your project.
  • Payments: card payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number or security code. We receive only limited information such as the payment status, card type and last four digits.
  • Technical data: our hosting and security providers log technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested.
  • Language and cookie preferences: the language you select and your cookie choice are saved in your browser.
  • Analytics and advertising data (only with your consent): if you allow it in the cookie banner, Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel collect the pages you view, how you arrived (for example from an ad or a campaign link), when you click an email, WhatsApp or Instagram link, that the contact form was sent successfully, and technical data such as your IP address, browser, device and cookie identifiers. The contents of the contact form (your name, email address and message) are never sent to Google or Meta.

3. How we use your data

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote.
  • To deliver the services you ordered and to issue invoices.
  • To meet our accounting and legal obligations.
  • To keep our website secure and prevent abuse.
  • Only with your consent: to measure visits to our website and the results of our ads, and to show our ads on Facebook and Instagram to people who have visited our website.

We use analytics and advertising cookies only on the basis of your consent, which you can withdraw at any time with the “Cookie preferences” link at the bottom of every page. See our Cookie Policy for the cookies, their purposes and durations.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not send marketing emails unless you ask us to.

4. Who we share data with

We share personal data only with the service providers we need to run our business:

  • Stripe, for payment processing (see the Stripe Privacy Policy).
  • Cloudflare, for content delivery and website security.
  • Our hosting provider, whose servers are located in the European Union.
  • Our email providers, to send and receive email.
  • Google Fonts, which serves the fonts on this website; Google receives your IP address when the fonts load.
  • Only with your consent: Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC (Google Analytics), for website analytics (see the Google Privacy Policy).
  • Only with your consent: Meta Platforms Ireland Limited and Meta Platforms, Inc. (Meta Pixel), for measuring and showing our ads on Facebook and Instagram. Meta uses this data as an independent controller under the Meta Privacy Policy.

We may also disclose data when the law requires it. Some of these providers process data outside the United Arab Emirates, including in the European Union and the United States. Analytics and advertising data is transferred to Google and Meta, including to the United States, only if you consent to analytics or advertising cookies; these providers use safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

5. How long we keep data

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a project: up to 24 months.
  • Project, invoice and accounting records: for at least seven years, or longer where UAE law requires it.
  • Cookies: for the periods listed in our Cookie Policy. Google and Meta keep the data they receive under their own retention policies.

6. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your personal data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. To make a request, email info@eyessoftware.com. We reply within 30 days. We delete your data on request unless we are legally required to keep it, for example in invoices.

You can withdraw your consent to analytics and advertising cookies at any time with the “Cookie preferences” link at the bottom of every page. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before. If you are in the European Union or Turkey, you also have the right to object to processing and to complain to your data protection authority.

7. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), restrict access to personal data to the people who need it, and rely on established providers for payments and hosting.

8. Children

Our services are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last changed.
