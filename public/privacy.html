Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This document is available in English only.

1. Who we are

Eyes Software FZCO (“Eyes Software”, “we”, “us”) operates eyessoftware.com and is responsible for the personal data described in this policy.

2. Data we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email address and the message you send us.
  • Projects and invoices: your name, company name (if any), billing address, email address, phone number, tax registration number where applicable, and the details of your project.
  • Payments: card payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number or security code. We receive only limited information such as the payment status, card type and last four digits.
  • Technical data: our hosting and security providers log technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested.
  • Language preference: the language you select is saved in your browser.

3. How we use your data

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote.
  • To deliver the services you ordered and to issue invoices.
  • To meet our accounting and legal obligations.
  • To keep our website secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not send marketing emails unless you ask us to.

4. Who we share data with

We share personal data only with the service providers we need to run our business:

  • Stripe, for payment processing (see the Stripe Privacy Policy).
  • Cloudflare, for content delivery and website security.
  • Our hosting provider, whose servers are located in the European Union.
  • Our email providers, to send and receive email.
  • Google Fonts, which serves the fonts on this website; Google receives your IP address when the fonts load.

We may also disclose data when the law requires it. Some of these providers process data outside the United Arab Emirates, including in the European Union and the United States.

5. How long we keep data

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a project: up to 24 months.
  • Project, invoice and accounting records: for at least seven years, or longer where UAE law requires it.

6. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your personal data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. To make a request, email info@eyessoftware.com. We reply within 30 days. We delete your data on request unless we are legally required to keep it, for example in invoices.

7. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), restrict access to personal data to the people who need it, and rely on established providers for payments and hosting.

8. Children

Our services are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last changed.
