Cookie Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This document is available in English only.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. This page explains which ones eyessoftware.com, operated by Eyes Software FZCO, uses.

2. Cookies we use

  • lang — remembers the language you selected. It is a first-party cookie that lasts one year. The same preference is also saved in your browser’s local storage.
  • Cloudflare security cookies (for example __cf_bm or cf_clearance) — may be set by Cloudflare, our security provider, to tell real visitors from automated traffic. They are strictly necessary and short-lived.

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies. If that changes, we will update this page first.

3. Third parties

  • Fonts are served by Google Fonts, which does not set cookies on this website.
  • Card payments are made on pages hosted by Stripe, which sets its own cookies there under the Stripe cookie policy.

4. How to disable cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block the lang cookie, the website still works but may not remember your language.

5. More information

See our Privacy Policy for how we handle personal data.
