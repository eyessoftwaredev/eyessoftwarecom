Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This document is available in English only.

1. Overview

Eyes Software FZCO provides custom software services that are quoted and invoiced per project or per milestone. This policy explains what happens if you cancel a project and when a refund is due. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

2. Cancelling before work starts

If you cancel before we have started work on your project, we refund everything you have paid, in full.

3. Cancelling after work has started

  • Milestones that have been completed are not refundable.
  • Payments for parts of the project that have not been started are refunded.
  • For a milestone that is in progress when you cancel, we keep a fee in proportion to the work completed and refund the rest. The amount we keep is never less than 25% of that milestone’s fee. Completed work is determined from the deliverables shared up to that point.

4. How to request a refund

Send your request to info@eyessoftware.com with your name, your invoice number and the reason for the request. We review every request within 10 business days and reply by email.

5. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Card refunds are issued through Stripe and usually appear on your statement within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

6. Before opening a dispute

If something is wrong with a payment or a delivery, please contact us before opening a dispute (chargeback) with your bank or card issuer. Most issues can be resolved faster directly with us.

7. Defects in delivered work

Defects (bugs) in the delivered scope that you report within 30 days of delivery are fixed at no extra cost. New features and requests outside the agreed scope are quoted separately. See our Terms of Service.
